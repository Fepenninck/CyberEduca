# Avatares do CyberEduca+

Gerados com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen` (sem CLI). Imagens finais em `frontend/public/images/avatares/`, WebP de 320 × 320 pixels. Os identificadores já salvos no banco foram preservados.

## Guardião — escudo.webp

Prompt completo:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: one square profile avatar for CyberEduca+, a cybersecurity learning website. Create a premium polished 3D rendered CHARACTER portrait, NOT an icon or symbol, not a photo of a real person. Subject: 'Guardião', a confident young Black woman digital security guardian, short natural curls, navy tactical tech jacket with tasteful lime green accents, a small futuristic shoulder plate, warm intelligent expression. Composition: centered head and shoulders facing camera, character fills 85% of square, keep entire hair and shoulders in frame, clean silhouette that reads at 40px. Style: sophisticated stylized 3D game character, realistic fabric and skin materials but animated-feature proportions, softly sculpted face, appealing and professional, not childish. Background: solid dark navy #142236, subtle studio lighting, soft lime rim light, no gradients outside natural lighting, no busy scenery. No text, logos, watermark, UI, decorative circles, shields or floating icons. Output a single avatar image.
